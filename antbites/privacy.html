Antbites — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Antbites is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game and the services inside it collect.

What the game itself keeps

Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

The game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics: levels started and finished, coins earned and spent, rewards claimed. This data has no name, email or advertising ID. It helps us balance difficulty and fix problems. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ads (AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads)

You can watch optional video ads for in-game rewards. Ads are served by AppLovin MAX together with Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking when the game asks), IP address, device model and ad interactions, to show ads and measure them. If you are in the EEA or the UK, the game asks for your consent first. You can change it at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

  • AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
